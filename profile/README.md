## What Alder does

Most late B2B invoices aren't refusals. They're stuck behind a stalled approval, a portal rejection, a missing PO number, or an AP contact who left. Alder works on the cause, not on a reminder schedule.

| Workflow | What Alder does |
|---|---|
| **Collections** | Works the aging ledger account by account, follows up in the original email thread, and tracks every promise to pay. |
| **Approvals & AP portals** | Submits into Coupa, Ariba, and custom portals, fixes rejections, and chases the person who actually releases payment. |
| **Disputes & short-pays** | Pulls the PO, receipt, and contracted rate, then hands your analyst a decision with the evidence. |
| **Cash application** | Matches incoming payments to open invoices across bank files, emailed remittances, and PDF scans. |

## How it works

- **Read-only to start.** Alder observes your workflow before it writes anything.
- **You stay in control.** You set tone, escalation, and approval rules; anything customer-facing waits for your approval.
- **Your data stays yours.** Never used to train models. Deploys inside your VPC or cloud account when required.
- **Full audit trail.** Every action is logged, attributable, and reversible.

Works with Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Coupa, NetSuite, SAP, QuickBooks, Stripe, Sequence, Brex, Mercury, Chase, and Plaid.
